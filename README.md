# Discovering Goya

## Website Description
A website about Francisco de Goya and his artworks, with images and explanations that introduce visitors to his paintings.

## Purpose
The website aims to help visitors discover Goya’s work, understand the themes of his paintings and develop an interest in art.

## Target Audience
The website is intended for students and people interested in art who have little previous knowledge of Goya. The explanations use clear language suitable for beginners.

## Design Decisions
- Images of the artworks accompany the explanations so visitors can connect the information with what they see.
- Clear headings and navigation help visitors find the artworks they want to explore.
- Short texts and accessible language make the content easier to understand without specialist knowledge.
